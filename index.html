    Capacity (estimate)

    Capacity allows 5% for stairs and entrances on seated tiers and 10% for gangways on terraces. Sightline uses the C-value: how far each fan's eye sits above the eye line of the person in front, aimed at the nearest edge of play (the top of a raised ring or cage), or the set distance into the floor. A seat is restricted view when a pillar sits within 12° of its line to the centre of play. Emptying time assumes every exit flows at the set rate at once.
